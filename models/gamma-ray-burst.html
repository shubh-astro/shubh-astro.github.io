A long gamma-ray burst as artists' impressions render it: a collapsing massive star whose core has become a black hole and accretion torus, driving two narrow ultra-relativistic jets that drill out through the dying envelope and break free. Two narrow beams launched along the rotation axis at almost the speed of light.

Internal shocks

Rings where faster shells overrun slower ones — where the gamma rays are made.

Cooling flow

Packets running out along the beams, blue-white at the base and yellow to orange where the flow has cooled.

Dying envelope

The star's outer layers, drilled through at the poles and blowing apart.
